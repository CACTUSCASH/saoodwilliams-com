UNDER THE HOOD

Every byte
accounted for.

01

Decoders

Ethernet with 802.1Q, Linux SLL and raw IP; IPv4 with options and fragments, IPv6 extension headers, ARP, TCP options, UDP, ICMP and ICMPv6. IP, TCP, UDP and ICMP checksums are verified, including the pseudo-header.

02

Streams

Flows are keyed by 5-tuple. Sequence arithmetic survives the 2^32 wrap, late segments wait in a bounded buffer, retransmissions are compared byte for byte, and holes become explicit gaps.

03

Limits

IP fragments are flagged but not reassembled. TLS records are decoded up to the encrypted boundary; nothing is decrypted. HTTP/2 and QUIC are not parsed. Captures up to 256 MB, decoded in memory.
