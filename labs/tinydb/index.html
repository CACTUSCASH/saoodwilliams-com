    How it is built

    Every layer,
    hand written.

    01

    Storage

    A growable ArrayBuffer of 4 KiB pages behind an LRU buffer pool with pin counts. Page 0 holds the header, catalog root and free list.

    02

    B+tree

    Slotted pages with variable-length cells, byte-balanced splits, merge or borrow on underflow, a stable root page and memcomparable keys.

    03

    Durability

    Commits append full page images and a commit record, each with a CRC32. Recovery replays whole transactions and drops a torn tail.

    04

    Limits

    One writer, no MVCC or subqueries, joins in FROM order, rows under 1,000 bytes (no overflow pages), 53-bit integers. The disk is memory: reload and it is gone.
