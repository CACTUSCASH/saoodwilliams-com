The sequence matters.

    Ready. Play to begin an election, or step through one tick at a time.

    What to try

    Make the minority
    lose the argument.

    1. 01

      Establish a leader.

      Play until a candidate receives three votes. Submit a command and watch matching log entries become committed.

    2. 02

      Split the network.

      The split places the current leader with one other node. It can accept a write locally, but it cannot commit a new entry alone.

    3. 03

      Restore the links.

      The majority can elect a leader in a higher term. After reconnection, conflicting uncommitted entries are replaced while the committed prefix stays intact.
